1. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled anytime.
2. Cancelling stops renewal; access continues until end of the paid month.
3. Monthly fees are not refunded for partial months.
4. Annual subscriptions are eligible for a refund only within 14 days of purchase.
5. Annual refunds apply only if usage is below 10% of the plan’s included MAU.
6. If usage is 10% or more, the annual subscription is non-refundable.
7. MAU usage is measured as MAU consumed in the current billing period.
8. Add-ons are refundable only if unused and requested within 7 days of purchase.
9. Chargebacks result in immediate account suspension pending review.
