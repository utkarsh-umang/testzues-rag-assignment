1. AcmeCloud is a hosted analytics platform for small and mid-size teams.
2. It offers three plans: Starter, Pro, and Business.
3. Plans differ by monthly active users (MAU), data retention, and support level.
4. Billing can be monthly or annual. Annual is discounted but has special refund rules.
5. AcmeCloud sells add-ons: Extra MAU packs and Extended Retention.
6. Some features are region-limited for compliance reasons.
