1. MAU means unique active users in a calendar month.
2. MAU is computed from distinct user_ids seen in tracked events.
3. Usage dashboards update every 6 hours.
4. MAU packs increase the MAU limit for the current month only.
5. Unused MAU packs do not roll over.
6. Extended Retention add-on increases retention by 365 days.
7. Usage is considered below 10% for annual refunds if MAU consumed < 10% of included MAU.
8. Example: Pro includes 100k MAU; 9,999 MAU is below 10%.
9. If the dashboard is delayed, billing still uses the backend MAU computation.
